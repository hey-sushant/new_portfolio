RTU Predictive Maintenance: HVAC Fault Diagnosis and Health Analytics In progress

PythonPandasscikit-learnStreamlitpytest
  • End-to-end analysis of the public LBNL rooftop HVAC fault dataset: 81 scenario files across simulated, lab and field sources.
  • Cleaned sensor data with unit harmonisation, physical-range checks, and dropout and spike detection, then engineered 275–374 features per 60-minute window. The pipeline is covered by 100 pytest tests.
  • Trained gradient-boosting fault classifiers with leakage-safe validation (1-day embargo, day-grouped CV). On 6 simulated fault types, the model reached 0.78 macro F1 on fault severities never seen in training.
  • Built a 0–1 fault-severity health index, checked against simulated efficiency (COP) loss. Refrigerant-charge faults didn't generalise, and that limitation is documented.
  • Built a Streamlit dashboard for detection, diagnosis, health index and rule-based maintenance advice, with every value labelled as measured, predicted or threshold-based. TreeSHAP showed two of the top three signals reflect how faults were injected, so field-readiness checks come next.

Ride-Booking Analytics Dashboard

Power BIDAXPower Query
  • Six-page interactive Power BI dashboard (Overview, Vehicle Type, Revenue, Cancellation, Ratings, Summary) tracking bookings, cancellations, revenue, and driver and customer ratings, with date and vehicle-type slicers on every page.
  • Cleaned and modelled 21 raw booking fields in Power Query, including VTAT/CTAT, cancellation reasons and payment method.
